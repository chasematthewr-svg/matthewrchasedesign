About Me

Matthew R. Chase is a Chicago-based Theatrical Sound Designer & Production Manager.

Sound Design credits include:
REGIONAL/SOUND DESIGN: Assassins (Capital City Theater); Strangers On A Train (Meadow Brook Theatre).
CHICAGO/SOUND DESIGN: Diana, Tell Me On A Sunday, The Secret Garden, Never Better, The Sondheim Tribute Revue, Assassins, Baked: A New Musical! (Theo); Rock of Ages (Metropolis Performing Arts Centre); Fun Home, Sunday in the Park with George, Anything Goes, Ernest Shackleton Loves Me, Cabaret, Rent, Spring Awakening (Porchlight Music Theatre); Legally Blonde: The Musical, Little Shop of Horrors, Shrek the Musical, The Producers, Pippin (Music Theater Works); Who’s Holiday (Theater Wit); The House Without a Christmas Tree (Griffin Theatre Company); Elephant And Piggie's We Are in a Play (Marriott Theatre).

Production Manager Credits include:
Girls and Boys, The House Without a Christmas Tree, Marys Seacole, Heisenberg (Griffin Theatre Company); The Secret Garden, Never Better, A Little Night Music, The Sondheim Tribute Revue, Assassins, Baked!: A New Musical, Passing Strange, The Threepenny Opera (Theo); Inanimate, Who's Holiday!, Household Spirits, The Whistleblower, Hurricane Diane, Mr. Burns a post electric play (Theater Wit); Spring Awakening (Porchlight Music Theatre).
